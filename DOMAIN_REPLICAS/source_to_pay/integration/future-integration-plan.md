# Future Payouts integration

Apply `unified-graph-patch.json` only after the protected Payouts work is complete. Preserve the `source_to_pay` namespace and join domains through the source-type/status-channel contract in `payouts-boundary-contract.yaml`. The local boundary is evidence for the `vendor-payments` ↔ `rzp_tax_pay` seam only; integration must validate against the real Payouts/API-monolith balance, workflow, Passport identity, contact authorization, validation, and persistence behavior before promoting its fidelity.

The TDS journey is two phase. `add-tds-entry` creates or updates the monthly tax aggregate and, for a payout entity, attempts to tag the originating payout. It does not create the remittance payout. A later, explicit `Pay`/remittance operation performs source validation and creates the internal-contact payout through API-monolith internal routes. A separate payout callback then returns status to vendor-payments. In the executed replica, a processed callback reaches external `processing` and internal `money_loading_success`; it does not establish paid tax, filing completion, or an accounting-payout status event.

Future integration must preserve this causality and must not connect `add-tds-entry` directly to payout creation or claim accounting propagation without a separately observed producer path. Re-run the boundary contract against the integrated implementation, add mismatched merchant/source identity negatives and production validator cases, and retain the source's month guard and repeated-pay protection. The [production unknowns](../../../reports/source-to-pay-replica/PRODUCTION_UNKNOWNS.md) remain acceptance constraints until the owning systems provide that evidence.
