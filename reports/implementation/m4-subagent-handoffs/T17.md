## 3. Driver design

Per-merchant `setup` (idempotent): `provision_direct_merchant` + `self_check_direct_merchant` (fresh Direct
merchant, 29/29) → `ledger_da.ensure_da_parent_accounts` + `onboard_da_merchant` (7 DA parents + 6 sub-accounts)
→ `set_features(da_ledger_journal_writes=True, payout_service_txn_recon=False)` → register the xas-sim account
→ `ensure_basd_row` (BASD selectable for the real worker). Two merchants A and B; journeys are assigned to
distinct merchants to demonstrate tenant isolation (A: A/C/E/F; B: B/D/G) and webhook scoping is asserted per
journey (`webhooks_scoped_to_merchant`).

Each journey drives ONE coherent payout through the real chain and records `{request, observed rows/states,
timestamps, webhook deliveries, ledger journals, reservation snapshots, fidelity}`. Reuse split:
- A/B/C/D/F composed from library calls (kong create, mozart scenario, reservations via `_reservations/_settle_reservation`, real-worker statement ingest via bas_fixtures, xas match→PS link→DA ledger via the m4_xas_ledger helpers, webhooks via `_deliveries`).
- E reuses `m4_bas_ingest.Proof.step_f` verbatim (the REAL `VerifyPayoutFailedTransaction` guard on the FTS-direct route + the F-T10-1 relay comparison).
- G reuses `m4_xas_ledger.Proof.step_e` (credit→reversed) + `step_h` (wrong-amount→external negative control).
