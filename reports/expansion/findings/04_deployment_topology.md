# 04 — Deployment topology & infra inventory for adjacent domains
Sources (read-only clones): `kube-manifests`, `spinacode`, `terraform-kong`, `edge`, `devstack`, `alert-rules`. All paths are `repo/relative/path:line`. OBSERVED = seen in file; INFERRED = deduced; UNKNOWN = not resolvable from these repos.

## 1) Summary
1. `kube-manifests` has no per-service top level; the canonical deployed-service list is `templates/` (913 charts) + env value dirs (`prod/` 356, `cde/` 135) + the RazorpayX production **cell** `cells/rzpx/prod/rzpx-inrx-pd01/ap-south-1/{cde,ncde}/applications/` (9 CDE + 33 NCDE apps) (`kube-manifests/AGENTS.md:7-33`).
2. The RazorpayX cell is the strongest canonical list: CDE = asv, cfa, emv-3ds-server, hvault-mhq, mozart(+pagination,+whitelisted), payouts, validx; NCDE = abacus, banking-bridge, barricade, batch, beam, bvs, charge-collections, chotabeam, dashboard, frontend-graphql, fts, integrations-upi-sharp, master-onboarding, merchant-experience-service, onboarding, **opfin, opfin-compliance, opfin-demo**, pgos, razorassist, recon, recon-saas, relay, **tax-compliance, tax-compliance-vault**, ufh, x-account-statements, x-balances, **x-customer-payout-links, x-payroll-compute, x-payroll-flexible-benefits, x-salary-structure**, xperience.
3. **Payroll is the largest adjacent domain by deployed footprint**: opfin (repo `x-payroll`, PHP-style `run_cron` jobs incl. `ActualBankTransfer`, `BatchBankTransfer`, `ProcessMerchantPayoutsInNonCreatedState`), opfin-compliance (`x-payroll-compliance`, Temporal), x-payroll-compute (Temporal + Kafka SDI consumer), x-payroll-flexible-benefits (Kafka `employee-settings-sync`), x-payroll-hris, x-salary-structure, x-tna (Temporal Cloud), plus its own alert cell `rzpx-inpr-pd01` and Postgres `prod-aurora-postgres-{opfin,xpayroll}`.
4. **Tax compliance (TDS)** is a distinct service consuming payout events from SQS `prod-tax-compliance-payout-events` (a direct payouts→tax edge), with its own Vault and Postgres.
5. **Vendor payments / AR (invoices)**: vendor-payments has 11 workers (tds, initiate-tds, ocr, bank-statement-fetch, accounting-payouts…); `accounts-receivable` + `x-accounts-receivable` (hosted invoices at `invoices-x.razorpay.com`, payout/payment webhooks) are NEW nodes.
6. **Current-account onboarding**: master-onboarding (gRPC `rzp.master_onboarding.intent.v1`), onboarding (obs), bvs, x-ai-onboarding-agent, x-partner-kit; alerts under `#x-acquisition-alerts` (X Login/Signup/VA KYC/CA routes).
7. **Capital** runs in its own cluster `prod-capital/` (11 apps) with Kong callbacks; Capital *corporate cards* surface inside xperience (`/v1/xperience-edge/corporate-cards/*`) and the X frontend devstack chart bundles `capital_commit_id`/`capital_onboarding_commit_id`/`vendor_portal_image`.
8. Several name-matched services are **NOT RazorpayX**: `razorx` (Growth experimentation, `#platform_growth_alerts`), `wallet` (Issuing/gift cards, `@razorpay/gc_issuing`), `invoices` (PG no-code apps), `merchant_invoice` (PG pricing), `scrooge` (refunds), `settlements` (PG), `xbi-*` (cross-border import), and the fluxcd `razorpayx` clusters host bank-side products (cas-acs 3DS, cardholder-portal/tokenization for KVB/Canara/Equitas/IDFC, mandatehq).
9. `devstack` repo is the generic tool; the service catalogue with dependencies is `kube-manifests/helmfile/helmfile.yaml` (373 charts) — X releases declare **no** `link_services`, so inter-service deps are not declared there.
10. Repo names: only `x`, `frontend-x`, `xperience`, `x-balances`, `x-account-statements`, `x-attendance`, `x-payroll-commons`, `x-bank-statement-sync`, `banking-accounts`, `vendor-*`, `payouts`, `payout-links`, `fts`, `ledger`, `cfa` are visible in `gh_repos.txt`; `x-payroll`, `x-payroll-compliance`, `x-payroll-compute`, `x-salary-structure`, `x-payroll-hris`, `tax-compliance`, `business-verification-service`, `master-onboarding`, `accounts-receivable`, `x-customer-payout-links`, `capital-*` are OBSERVED as image/pipeline names but not listable.
